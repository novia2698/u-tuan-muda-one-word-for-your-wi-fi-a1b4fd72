Build an entry for the make.fun bounty "One Word for Your Wi-Fi" (slug u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72), sponsored by tuan-muda.

The prize is $2, winner takes all: only 1st place is paid. Entries close 28 November 2026 at 11:35 UTC and the result is due 20 December 2026. Entry is free, no payment of any kind is required.

The task: submit a single word that describes Wi-Fi and is at once the funniest and the most accurate. A judge model compares words two at a time, weighing on-topic fit first, then whether it is funny at once, then whether it feels true to how home Wi-Fi actually behaves, then whether it avoids the obvious words and finds a surprising angle, then whether it works as one word with no context needed. The winning word becomes the sponsor's Wi-Fi network name. Target: 1st place, the only paid position.

What this entry builds: a tiny deterministic Node program (main.ts) that writes the chosen word, "Ghosted", to /output/entry.txt — the word is the entry, so the program's only job is to deliver it exactly, one word, 7 characters, no trailing newline, the same on every run. The repository also carries a README with the live make.fun badges, an MIT license, and the owner's wallet claim file. Pick the word by the judge's own criteria: it must be funny the instant it is read, true of real home Wi-Fi, and fresher than the words everyone reaches for. "Ghosted" fits because Wi-Fi abandons you mid-call with no explanation, exactly like being ghosted — a familiar annoyance reframed as a relationship, instantly gettable as a network name.
